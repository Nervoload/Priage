# Clinic Care handoff

What Care shows a clinic clinician before they walk into the room, where each part comes from, and how a model may take over parts of it later. Clinics only. The ED workflow is unchanged.

## Principles

- **No CTAS in clinics.** Clinic Care never shows, stores or derives a CTAS level. Urgency is a clinic-only rating with three levels: **Clear**, **Caution** and **Escalate**. It is raised only by high-risk answers that could progress to something an emergency department should handle.
- **Three voices, always labelled.** Patient words, text Priage generated, and clinician text are styled and labelled differently on screen and in every copy or export.
- **Decision support only.** Everything generated sits under one label: "Generated by Priage from the answers. Check it against them." Nothing generated is phrased as a diagnosis.
- **Frozen evidence.** A Care snapshot is immutable once Care starts, because comments point at exact passages in it. While a visit is still waiting, a snapshot built by older rules is replaced with a new version.

## The snapshot (schema version 2)

Built by `backend/src/modules/clinic/care/care-snapshot.ts` from the interview's state chain, its answer records, the patient's own start-form words (`patient_intake`) and the patient profile. It keeps every v1 field so older screens still render.

| Part | Source | Voice | Owner who signs it off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Briefing, case summary | Templates in `clinic-handoff.rules.ts` (model summary only when the interview ran in AI mode) | Generated | Clinical owner |
| Urgency and its reasons | Rules (`clinic-handoff-rules@N`) | Generated | Clinical owner |
| Red flags, red-flag screen | Rules: negation-aware keyword domains over the patient's words only | Generated | Clinical owner |
| Emergency warnings | Derived from the interview state chain (shown and continued times) | Record | — |
| Next steps | Templates per urgency level | Generated | Clinical owner |
| Ask in the room (max 6, must-ask first) | Rules: clarify vague answers, unscreened red flags, missing medications/allergies, sensitive topics, exam re-checks, clinic travel answer | Generated | Clinical owner |
| Not established (gaps) | Rules: not screened, vague answers, not asked, question limit, unfinished, emergency continued, clinic questions missing | Generated | Clinical owner |
| Answers | Interview answer records: safety question, clinic questions, then assessment questions by stage, each with "why we asked" | Patient (answers), record (questions) | — |
| Visit record | Start form and profile | Patient | — |
| Considerations, exam suggestions, timed risks | Empty for now; storage and UI exist and stay hidden until filled | Generated | Clinical owner |

Every commentable passage is a segment with an id, voice, section and (for generated text) the rule id that produced it. Headings and labels are never segments, which is what keeps them uncommentable.

### Urgency rules (`clinic-handoff-rules@1`)

- **Escalate:** said Yes to the immediate-danger question; saw a model emergency warning; reported a high-risk domain (breathing, chest, neurological signs, heavy bleeding, fainting, severe allergic reaction, self-harm); or severity 9–10.
- **Caution:** severity 7–8; worse with activity; sudden onset; dehydration; spreading redness; high fever; dizziness; pregnancy with pain or bleeding.
- **Clear:** none of the above. It lists what was denied.
- A rating from an unfinished assessment says so.

Matching reads only the patient's words: complaint, their note, answers, profile. A mention is "denied" when a negation appears earlier in the same clause. Curly apostrophes from phones are treated as straight ones. Rule wording and keyword lists need clinical-owner sign-off before the pilot.

## Clinic questions

- Each clinic publishes versions of its own questions in Settings (`ClinicQuestionnaireVersion`, immutable). Only admins and clinical admins can publish. Publishing checks the version the admin edited, so two admins can't overwrite each other.
- The safety question stays in the interview engine and is always first. The clinic's questions are pinned into the interview when it starts and asked **right after the safety question**, before the assessment's own questions. They don't count toward the assessment's question budget, and a running interview keeps the version it started with.
- Patients who finished an assessment on the general Priage site and then chose the clinic answer the clinic's questions separately, before booking (`clinic_questionnaire_response` context item). Booking is refused until they do.
- Care shows clinic answers in their own group with the version and when they were asked. Reception sees only whether they were answered.
- The one Priage default is travel outside Canada in the last 14 days. Clinics can reword or remove it. A Yes adds an "Ask in the room" item while the question keeps its key.

## Clinician feedback

- Physicians and clinical admins can mark any generated item **Useful** or **Not right** (with an optional note), and leave a **Something missing** note on a section. Each clinician sees only their own feedback in Care.
- Each verdict stores the snapshot, segment, section, rule id and generator version (`CareAiFeedback`), so feedback stays tied to the exact rules or prompt that produced the item.
- Ticking an "Ask in the room" item records it as an asked clinician question. The first answer typed in is added to the visit note as clinician text.
- Clinic admins see totals in **Analytics**: urgency mix, emergency warnings, ask-in-room use, clinic-question completion, feedback by section, rule and generator version, and recent notes. Every Analytics read is audited.

## Intended model use (future pass)

The full design for the model-led assessment and its handoff writer is in [AI assessment: vision and principles](AI_ASSESSMENT_VISION.md) and [AI assessment harness](AI_ASSESSMENT_HARNESS.md).

Today clinics run the deterministic path only. When a regional model adapter is approved:

1. **Storage.** The model writes a `clinic_handoff` context item after the interview completes, outside interview lock 74001, behind a `CLINIC_HANDOFF_MODE` flag (`off`, `shadow`, `on`). In shadow mode it is stored and scored but not shown.
2. **What it may fill:**
   - possible considerations, each citing answers for and against, never phrased as a diagnosis;
   - focused exam suggestions with a reason and answer references;
   - timed risks ("watch for") once the clinical owner approves a risk table;
   - extra ask-in-room items.

   Every item must reference segment ids that exist in the snapshot. Items that don't are dropped.
3. **Urgency floor.** The model may raise urgency above the deterministic level, never lower it. The rules' red flags and emergency events always show.
4. **Prompt.** A clinic-specific prompt, separate from the ED prompt. Clinic answers are already in the interview answers it receives. The ED prompt must stay byte-identical.
5. **Evaluation.** Use the Analytics feedback, split by generator version, to compare the model with the rules (useful share, not-right rate by section and rule, missing notes), along with clinician review of the recent notes. A version that does worse than the rules on any high-risk section doesn't ship.
6. **Snapshot ids for model items:** `consider:N`, `exam:N`, `exam:N:why`, and the timed-risk id. The UI already renders and takes feedback on these.
