# Clinic pilot: current state and delivery plan

Status: updated on 2026-09-30. The repository now includes the identity/contact/legal foundation, gated clinic intake, appointment preview, tenant-aware entry, an internal clinic Care workspace, and appointment communications with separately verified scheduling recovery. A pinned `CLINIC_APPOINTMENT` API or an internal general preview accepts a completed assessment plus an available slot and affirmative acceptance of the current published Terms and Privacy versions, then moves `INTAKE` to `REQUESTED`. Reception alone confirms the slot and moves the visit to `EXPECTED`; arrival and clinician handoff lead to `ADMITTED` and `CARE`, then completion closes the encounter and appointment together. Local mock PostgreSQL migrations and intake, booking, general-entry, and Care smoke checks passed. This is **not** Azure staging or public launch sign-off. The communications slice has local captured email delivery and an optional restricted Resend adapter; real provider delivery and Azure staging are not signed off. Approved legal copy, clinic questionnaire and approved Canadian model-backed assessment remain. See the [communications rollout](../backend/docs/CLINIC_COMMUNICATIONS_PREVIEW_ROLLOUT.md). The [implementation blueprint](./CLINIC_PILOT_IMPLEMENTATION_BLUEPRINT.md) tracks remaining scope.

## Pilot journey

Patient starts a visit request (with or without an account) → completes the AI assessment and clinic questionnaire → requests an available appointment → receptionist reviews and confirms the time → encounter becomes **Expected** and confirmation/reminders are emailed → patient arrives and moves through **Reception** → physician opens **Care**, reviews the assessment, writes notes, and copies selected content into the clinic EMR → encounter is completed. A walk-in can be registered at Reception without an appointment.

The Waiting Room screen is hidden for this tenant. It remains available for other workflows; hiding it must not cause an encounter to disappear from all staff queues. Post-visit follow-up is a later delivery slice of this plan.

### Patient entry choices in the gated preview

- A Priage-owned `/{alias}/start` link resolves its clinic on the API before patient intake. A website button can use this URL. Renaming an alias keeps the old alias resolving to the current path. A Reception desk grant uses `/{alias}/walk-in#token=…`; the API checks that the one-use grant belongs to that clinic. The URL is discoverability, not an invitation credential.
- A listed clinic appears in the general patient directory only on an internal API deployment that explicitly allowlists it. An unlisted clinic remains reachable by its direct link. The general funnel completes assessment before destination selection; choosing a clinic attaches the draft to that clinic, while choosing an ED follows the existing ED confirmation path. An appointment-only clinic blocks arrived walk-in registration independently of directory listing.
- Patient-initiated ED and clinic intake require a visit contact email, including for account holders. Reception-created walk-ins may omit it. The clinic booking page uses an authoritative visit-state response, applies command results, and refreshes from encounter events, focus/reconnect and a polling fallback. Reception No-show appears only after a confirmed slot ends. Schedule editing supports multiple windows per day and date-specific custom hours.
- Direct clinic guest intake now uses the same patient start form and assessment question screen as the ED guest funnel. Signed-in patients see the same account summary and visit fields as in general intake; their visit contact email is editable and remains separate from login identity. A direct link binds the clinic at visit start and skips destination selection. The general route retains its post-assessment directory; selecting a clinic opens booking without repeating the completed assessment summary. Both routes load booking availability and legal documents through the patient-owned visit, so a changed or forged path alias cannot choose another clinic's slots. The desk QR route gives assessment-only access to a Reception-created walk-in. Listing and walk-in acceptance are independent clinic settings, so an appointment-only clinic may be listed and a walk-in clinic may remain unlisted.

## Recommended product model

1. The gated preview has an appointment record scoped to clinic and encounter: requested/confirmed slot, timezone, duration, status (`REQUESTED`, `CONFIRMED`, `DECLINED`, `CANCELLED`, `EXPIRED`, `NO_SHOW`, `COMPLETED`), actor/timestamps, revision and idempotency key. Visit contact email is separate and linked through the encounter. Keep appointment state distinct from physical encounter stage.
2. Use the implemented `INTAKE` stage while assessment is underway, then `REQUESTED` only after a required slot request is submitted. Clinic path: `INTAKE` → `REQUESTED` → `EXPECTED` (receptionist confirms appointment) → `ADMITTED` (arrival) → `CARE` → `COMPLETE`. Walk-ins enter at `ADMITTED` without an appointment. Preserve the ED path and migrate every status reader deliberately.
3. Add a tenant workflow profile with visible stages and labels. For the pilot, pin the Patient App to the clinic **on the server as well as in the UI**, hide `WAITING`, and allow physician access to `CARE` after arrival. Keep the first implementation an explicit clinic profile instead of a general workflow builder.
4. Scheduling rules and server-calculated availability are in the gated preview: clinic timezone, opening hours, slot duration, capacity, date closures and blocks. Slot requests and confirmation are transactional; a request expires after the configured hold and releases capacity. Clinic owners must approve the actual hours, capacity and request policy.
5. Store contact email on the visit/appointment independently of account identity. Record whether it was supplied by the patient or receptionist, verify or clearly label unverified addresses, and do not use an appointment link as a patient account credential. Guests need a scoped, expiring way to view or change an appointment after their current session expires; require fresh email verification for sensitive changes. Keep notifications brief and avoid complaint details in email.
6. Make the AI output an immutable, versioned assessment snapshot with a stable question/answer sequence. Keep patient answers, AI summary, and clinician notes clearly attributed. Store a physician note and anchored comments as separate, audited records; anchors reference stable assessment segment IDs and snapshot version, not raw character offsets alone. Explicit copy buttons can format a summary, Q&A, or note for PS-SUITE; record copy action only if the audit policy requires it, never clipboard contents.
7. Version clinic questions and persist answers against the encounter and question version. Required types for the pilot: yes/no, one-of-up-to-four choices with optional “Other”, and short text with a server-enforced 300-character limit. Capture terms/privacy document versions and acceptance timestamp at the final visit confirmation step; publish the same links in account settings.
8. Clinic model context should be a separate, versioned admin setting with exact text display, author/time, and audit history. Inject it as bounded clinic context, with prompt/version tracking and clinical review before activation. It must not silently override emergency escalation rules.

## Implemented communications and appointment recovery

Confirmation commits with its outbox and future reminders. Rescheduling invalidates reminders and requires Reception confirmation again; previously confirmed bookings receive change-pending or cancellation notices. Arrival, cancellation, No-show and completion stop outstanding reminders. Admin Settings controls tenant delivery, Reply-To, phone and up to two offsets. Reception displays delivery separately from scheduling; provider acceptance is not proof of delivery or reading. A database sweep behind BullMQ recovers pending work; frozen payloads and stable keys protect retries, with ambiguous or near-expired idempotency outcomes sent to review.

Management links carry an opaque booking reference with no permissions. Visit-email verification grants a separate 24-hour scheduling-only cookie; codes expire in ten minutes with five attempts, cooldowns and email/IP limits. Changes require verification within ten minutes and use the existing revision/idempotency checks. Contact correction increments its version and revokes prior recovery. The patient page reuses booking controls and refreshes automatically. Local captured recovery material is encrypted and removed on use/expiry; actual provider codes are never shown in staff history.

Resend is the selected first adapter. Recipient addresses, clinic association, scheduling content, temporary codes and delivery events are an explicitly approved external US data flow, while the application, PostgreSQL and workers remain on Azure in Canada. Emails omit names, complaints, assessments and health identifiers. [Resend storage policy](https://resend.com/security). Restricted test delivery requires an exact recipient allowlist; live delivery requires a verified sender with tracking disabled, signed callbacks and documented clinic approval. Public entry remains gated. The next product implementation is the configurable clinic questionnaire; model assessment remains a separate task.

## Pilot release scenarios

Run these with the clinic's actual hours and timezone, without real patient data until the earlier identity backfill and staging controls are complete:

- Guest completes assessment and clinic questions, requests a slot, sees **pending** status, and receives no confirmation email yet; receptionist confirms and the encounter becomes Expected.
- Two patients request the last available slot at the same time; capacity is respected. A declined, expired, cancelled, or rescheduled request releases capacity correctly.
- A confirmed guest receives one confirmation and the configured reminders, can identify the correct visit after the initial guest session expires, and cannot see another patient's encounter.
- Reception records an arrived walk-in without a shared login credential or mandatory email. The patient can use a one-use desk code, or staff can enter answers in person. Routine Care handoff requires completion; an authorized clinician may override for urgent care with an audited reason. No-show and cancellation have distinct appointment outcomes in the gated preview; review the clinic's actual policies in staging.
- Physician reviews the source Q&A and AI summary, writes and reopens a note, copies selected content to a test EMR field, and sees an obvious distinction between reported facts, AI text, and clinician-authored text.
- Assessment generation, email delivery, realtime updates, and browser refresh each fail in turn; the patient/staff screens show an accurate recoverable state and do not claim a booking or clinical review that did not occur.
- In the restricted general preview, an unlisted clinic is absent from search but its direct link works; a listed clinic accepts a completed general intake draft, retaining answers and contact. Changing the alias preserves old website and desk links. A forged tenant ID and a desk grant for another clinic are rejected.
- On an appointment-only clinic, Reception cannot register a walk-in. Multiple daily and custom-date hours are editable; No-show appears and succeeds only after the confirmed slot ends. A stale patient or Reception command receives current visit state and can recover without a manual status refresh.

## Security and clinical constraints for the pilot

- Keep the assessment as decision support requiring clinician review; do not present AI content as a verified chart fact. Review clinic-specific prompts and emergency escalation with the clinical owner before enabling them.
- Use tenant-scoped authorization on every appointment, assessment, note, question, and contact endpoint. Do not rely on hidden navigation or a hard-coded frontend clinic slug for isolation.
- Keep encounter data authoritative on the server. Prefer short-lived in-memory UI state and targeted refetching; do not add persistent browser caching of PHI or images until device management, encryption, logout purge, expiry, and shared-workstation behavior are defined. Remove or tightly scope the existing triage `localStorage` draft before the pilot.
- The existing deletion flow retains clinic visit records. Do not promise “deleted within 90 days” until the actual retention schedule and legal/clinic requirements support that statement.
- Verify the deployment gates in `docs/PRODUCTION_OPERATIONS.md` and the clinical release gates in `docs/CLINICAL_GOVERNANCE.md` with pilot-specific evidence. Written runbooks are not a launch sign-off.
- Internal deterministic assessment is labelled as a preview and must remain behind `CLINIC_PREVIEW_ENABLED` plus Azure staging access restrictions. Booking mechanics are implemented, but public clinic entry stays disabled until approved/published Terms and Privacy copy, Canadian model deployment controls, notifications and the remaining pilot gates are complete.

## Decisions to settle with the pilot clinic

1. Appointment slot length, capacity, business hours, timezone, holidays, and whether a request temporarily holds a slot.
2. Whether reception can change a requested time, how the patient confirms a change, and when a stale request expires.
3. Exact fields and format copied into PS-SUITE, and whether there is any authorized integration beyond manual paste.
4. Which appointment and follow-up emails are permitted, from what address, with what wording and unsubscribe/contact rules; who handles delivery failures.
5. Required clinic questionnaire, consent/terms/privacy text and versions, and approved retention wording.
6. Who may edit clinic AI context, who approves it, and which assessment/notification metrics signal a pause or rollback.
